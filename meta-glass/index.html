Why 3024 × 4032 resolution? ▼

Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 2) Smart Glasses feature a 12-Megapixel ultra-wide sensor producing exact 3:4 portrait photos at 3024 x 4032 px. Our canvas engine calculates smart aspect-fill to crop centered without distortion.

What EXIF tags are injected? ▼

The studio injects hardware tags: Make: Meta, Model: Ray-Ban Meta 2, Software: Meta View 151.0, plus focal length and lens specs required by Meta AI.

How does video conversion work? ▼

Videos are rendered frame-by-frame on an HTML5 canvas at 1440x1920 (Ray-Ban Meta portrait format) or 1184x1184 (Stories 1:1), while Web Audio API captures and multiplexes the original audio track into the output MP4/WebM.
